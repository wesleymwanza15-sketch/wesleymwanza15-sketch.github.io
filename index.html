My story

My name is Ben Wesley Mwanza—though around here, you might know me as phoenix_view_bwm, or simply the passing photographer.

I am a traveler, a writer, a software architect, and a visual storyteller. Recognized as a nominee for Best Documentary Photography at the 2026 Creative Media Awards (CMA Zambia), I operate at the intersection of creative lens production and hard-coded digital infrastructure.

Someone once told me;

"The version of you today isn't the version you will be tomorrow."

That thought stayed with me. It made me wonder: How do we hold onto who we are right now? How do we keep today alive for the person we become tomorrow?

Then I looked at the camera and the code editor, and asked myself: How can I combine these two worlds?

That moment birthed my answer. Welcome to a world of frozen frames and digital systems—preserved through the View of Ben Wesley Mwanza.
