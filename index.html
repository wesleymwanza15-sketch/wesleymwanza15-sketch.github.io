My story

My name is Ben Wesley Mwanza—though around here, you might know me as phoenix_view_bwm, or simply the passing photographer.

I am a traveler, a writer, a software architect, and a visual storyteller. Recognized as a nominee for Best Documentary Photography at the 2026 Creative Media Awards (CMA Zambia), I operate at the intersection of creative lens production and hard-coded digital infrastructure.

Someone once told me;

"The version of you today isn't the version you will be tomorrow."

That thought stayed with me. It made me wonder: How do we hold onto who we are right now? How do we keep today alive for the person we become tomorrow?

Then I looked at the camera and the code editor, and asked myself: How can I combine these two worlds?

That moment birthed my answer—and expanded Phoenix View Media into an open creative internet archive where photographers, models, filmmakers, scriptwriters, animators, and authors can showcase their craft and collaborate together.

Full Media & Grant Profile (140 Words)

Ben Wesley Mwanza—known creatively as phoenix_view_bwm and "the passing photographer"—is a Zambian Visual Archivist & Developer operating at the intersection of documentary lens production and front-end software architecture. Through his creative media brand, Phoenix View Media, he documents overlooked ecosystems, rural transit corridors, and cultural stories across Zambia, including acclaimed photo-editorial series from Chipata Forest, Chadiza, and Victoria Falls. A shortlisted nominee for Best Documentary Photography at the 2026 Creative Media Awards in Lusaka, Mwanza also authors original screenplays and documentary pitch bibles, including The Frontline Keepers: Zambia's Wildlife War and Game of Couples, alongside interactive digital publications and web systems.
