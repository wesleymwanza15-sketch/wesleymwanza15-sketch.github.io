My story

My name is Ben Wesley Mwanza—though around here, you might know me as phoenix_view_bwm, or simply the passing photographer.

I'm a traveler, a writer, a developer, and a visual storyteller. But more than anything, I'm someone obsessed with the beauty of time.

Someone once told me, "The version of you today isn't the version you will be tomorrow."

That thought stayed with me. It made me wonder: How do we hold onto who we are right now? How do we keep today alive for the person we become tomorrow?

Then I saw the camera.

It hit me—the power to freeze time was right there waiting. I grabbed my phone, opened the camera, and started shooting. I realized that if I could direct my lens right into those fleeting moments, I could freeze them forever. That way, whenever we miss a feeling, we can always wander back to the exact second it was captured and feel it all over again.

And here we are.

Welcome to a world of frozen frames—moments preserved through the View of Ben Wesley Mwanza and the spirit of the Phoenix. As a passing photographer, I'm just here capturing the journey... and maybe next, capturing a piece of yours.
